# Stage21 SPA diagnostic — 2026-09-02

Target: `momentum-stage21-sbi-2026-09-v1` / Stage21 rounded v1

This is a same-sample data-snooping diagnostic. It does not change Production, the frozen backtest, or the True Forward OOS clock.

## Question

Does the apparent historical performance survive a multiple-model correction when Stage21 is considered together with nearby allocations and key structural ablations?

## Sample and implementation

- sample: 2020-01-01 through 2026-08-25
- observations: 1,669 daily returns
- frozen Stage21 CAGR: 48.6072%
- frozen Stage21 MaxDD: -16.8860%
- bootstrap replications: 5,000
- stationary-bootstrap expected block lengths: 5, 10, 20 trading days
- HAC studentization lag: 10
- shared bootstrap indices across all candidates preserve cross-model dependence
- Hansen SPA-style consistent recentering

Candidate family: 32 models.

- 27 coarse allocation neighbors: 3 NORMAL × 3 YELLOW × 3 DEEP allocations
- frozen Stage21 rounded v1
- Stage21 without CFTC
- Stage21 without M3
- Stage21 without both CFTC and M3
- Stage21 with GLDM replaced by Cash

The candidate family is intentionally local. It does not claim to reconstruct every architecture ever examined during the full research path.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 sessions | 0.0164 | 2.0295 | 0.0336 |

| 20 sessions | 0.0178 | 2.0295 | 0.0354 |

At the 5% level, the frozen Stage21 model remains significantly above QQQ in mean daily return after correcting against this 32-model local family. The conclusion is stable across the three bootstrap block lengths.

The model producing the family maximum T statistic was `Stage21-no-CFTC-no-M3`, not frozen Stage21. This is expected from a raw-return objective: removing defensive overlays can increase mean return while materially worsening drawdown. Prior ablation showed the no-CFTC/no-M3 structure at approximately -26.18% MaxDD, so the SPA winner is not the Production objective winner.

## Raw-return SPA results versus Fixed60

For all three block lengths:

- global SPA p = 1.0000
- Stage21 T = -1.8341
- Stage21 family-wise adjusted p = 1.0000

No model in this local family has superior mean daily return to Fixed60. This is also expected: over the frozen sample, Fixed60 CAGR is approximately 62.0%, versus Stage21 48.61%, while Fixed60 MaxDD is approximately -31.13% versus Stage21 -16.89%.

Therefore this test must not be interpreted as a rejection of Stage21. Stage21 was selected for a return/drawdown objective, not for unconstrained maximum mean return.

## Interpretation

This result materially reduces the concern that Stage21's QQQ-relative historical edge is merely the best outcome from a small local allocation search. The evidence is consistent across plausible bootstrap dependence lengths.

However, it does **not** remove the main remaining selection-bias issue identified in the validation summary: many different architectures were explored on the same 2020–2026 sample before Stage21 was chosen. A local 32-model SPA cannot correct for models outside its candidate family.

Current assessment after this diagnostic:

- exact/local parameter overfit risk: **low**
- local data-snooping risk within the tested Stage21 neighborhood: **low**
- architecture-selection bias across the full research path: **meaningful / medium**
- True Forward evidence: **not yet sufficient**

## Limitation of raw-return SPA

Classical SPA uses a scalar performance differential. Mean return is useful as a standard diagnostic, but it does not encode the Stage21 research constraint that drawdown should remain near or inside the ~17% historical boundary. A raw-return SPA will naturally prefer less-defensive variants when they earn more return despite substantially larger drawdowns.

A further same-sample test should therefore focus on reconstructing the major historical architecture candidates and applying a pre-specified risk-aware comparison rule. The rule must be fixed before viewing those results to avoid introducing a new optimization layer.

Even a broader architecture-level SPA cannot recover candidate models that were tried but never committed. True Forward OOS from 2026-09-02 remains the decisive evidence.
