## Frozen source semantics

The strict Series-level acceptance and binding semantics remain exactly the validated H1-2008 semantics:

- candidate-prefilter evidence is discovery only and is not final ETF evidence;
- prior candidate CIKs may carry forward only at the candidate layer;
- prior positive Series acceptance is not inherited merely because a candidate carries forward;
- Series/Class IDs are usable only when contemporaneously public; no future identity backfill;
- final positive Series acceptance requires contemporaneously public issuer-own operational ETF evidence plus the frozen structural Series/Class binding rules;
- broad creation-unit or exchange language is candidate-prefilter evidence only;
- references to investments in third-party ETFs are not issuer-own operational evidence;
- registrant/trust name may not substitute for Series identity;
- trust-global sibling binding is prohibited;
- conventional mutual-fund siblings are excluded unless that Series independently satisfies the frozen ETF evidence and binding rules;
- no fuzzy/edit-distance identity repair;
- no ticker, holdings, ranking, return, or strategy-outcome inference in source identification;
- complete-portfolio form coverage remains `N-Q`, `N-Q/A`, `N-CSR`, `N-CSR/A`, `N-CSRS`, `N-CSRS/A` plus already accepted later forms when applicable;
- an amendment replaces a source only when the amendment itself contains the complete portfolio schedule;
- `sourceNoSchedule` is audit-only and may not enter `sourceOccurrences`.

## Required checks for PASS

1. **Cumulative inventory replay**
   - H2 cumulative N-Q inventory reproduces every H1 cumulative N-Q row through `2008-06-30` exactly.
   - H2 cumulative complete-portfolio inventory reproduces every H1 cumulative complete-portfolio row through `2008-06-30` exactly.
   - Any replay drift is a hard stop.

2. **Candidate recall only**
   - every H1-2008 candidate CIK remains in the merged H2 candidate-review set;
   - carry-forward affects candidate review eligibility only, never final Series acceptance;
   - a newly discovered H2 candidate cannot revise any closed source snapshot.

3. **Closed positive-Series replay**
   - all prior positive Series identities and binding methods reproduce under evidence public by the relevant closed signal date;
   - later H2 Series/Class metadata or prospectus evidence may not repair a closed-period identity.

4. **Closed source-snapshot replay**
   - all 30 closed signal snapshots from `2006-01` through `2008-06` reproduce exactly at `sourceFilings` level;
   - authoritative closed source snapshots must be assembled from their validated source artifacts, not inferred from a later artifact unless exact containment has itself been verified;
   - no H2 filing, identity, ETF evidence item, or newly discovered candidate may leak into a closed snapshot.

5. **H2 period/cardinality and PIT**
   - exactly the six fixed H2 signal dates are produced;
   - every source occurrence and operational ETF evidence item is public by its applicable signal date;
   - `dateFiled <= asOf` for all admitted evidence.

6. **Strict-source integrity**
   - Series identity conflicts are zero;
   - source/prospectus fetch or parse errors that would make the catalog incomplete are zero;
   - every accepted source occurrence has a complete portfolio schedule under frozen semantics;
   - every newly accepted Series satisfies the same issuer-own evidence and structural binding rules as H1-2008;
   - unauthorized sibling admission is zero;
   - `sourceNoSchedule` remains audit-only.

7. **No strategy leakage**
   - no Stage21 return, rank, CAGR, MaxDD, Calmar, trade, portfolio outcome, or later strategy behavior may be consulted to accept, reject, repair, or tune a Series, filing, identity, evidence rule, or source snapshot.

Any inventory replay, candidate carry-forward, identity, closed-source replay, PIT, parser, or source-integrity failure is a stop condition. Audit implementation or evidence lineage only; do not relax or tune the frozen methodology after results are known.
