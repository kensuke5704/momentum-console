## Structural parser issues identified

Two non-performance issues were confirmed:

1. Some N-PX records expose placeholder ticker text such as `N/A`. The old regex could reduce this to `N`, creating a false ticker identity.
2. Issuer names can differ only by leading/trailing `THE`, e.g. `PROCTER AND GAMBLE CO THE` vs `Procter & Gamble Co.`.

Automatic fuzzy matches remain prohibited. ADR suffixes are not stripped because an ADR and an ordinary share can be distinct securities.
