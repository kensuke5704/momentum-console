## Critical correction: old nine-series PIT sample is superseded

The earlier N-Q segmentation treated every `SCHEDULE OF INVESTMENTS (CONTINUED)` marker as a separate candidate portfolio and assigned pages using holdings-word overlap. This was structurally wrong. Continuation pages could map to unrelated ETFs when an industry word overlapped a series name.

Therefore:
- old PIT artifact `9854510485` is superseded;
- the old nine-series sample is invalid as historical-universe evidence;
- the old 43.53% count / 60.67% weight mapping result is mapping-engine history only.

No strategy returns were inspected in discovering or correcting this issue.

## Legacy US structural gate

Schedule-level diagnostic run `33714585732`, artifact `9878045597` found explicit country allocations only in DGT among the examined portfolios. DGT reported United States 62.7%, with United Kingdom, Switzerland, Japan, France and others also present.

SEC Form N-PORT Item C.5 defines the primary country field as the country where the issuer is organized. Therefore:
- US listing venue is not enough;
- numeric CUSIP is not enough;
- absence of ADR/GDR is not enough;
- ADR/GDR is useful foreign evidence but not a complete country classifier;
- explicit country sections can be used when present;
- otherwise country remains UNKNOWN pending an independent issuer-country source.

The next scalable US route should key issuer organization country to the mapped issuer/ticker/security ID rather than hand-label individual desired securities.
