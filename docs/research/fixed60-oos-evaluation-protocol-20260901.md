# Fixed60 True Forward OOS evaluation protocol — 2026-09-01

## Purpose

Pre-register how Fixed60 True Forward OOS will be interpreted before meaningful OOS performance accumulates. This protocol does not change the trading rule or the previously frozen Kill Criteria.

## Clock and sample

- Rule freeze: 2026-08-30.
- First eligible signal: 2026-08-31 US close.
- First eligible execution: next US open after that signal.
- Only post-freeze observations generated by the frozen Production strategy count as True Forward OOS.
- Provisional close rows may be replaced by completed adjusted rows; confirmed dates must not be rewritten.

## Evaluation hierarchy

### 0–3 months: implementation integrity only

Do not judge CAGR. Review only:

- signal date equals the strategy clock date;
- Point-in-Time Universe is available by that date;
- Top2 and 60/40 weights match the frozen rule;
- orders are placed at next US open, not same close;
- 10 bp/side cost is applied consistently;
- Stop 17.5%, Circuit 15%, and Recovery10 state transitions match the engine;
- live state, dashboard, backtest engine, and OOS record remain strategy-id consistent;
- no confirmed OOS history is rewritten.

Immediate MaxDD Review/Kill thresholds remain active during this period.

### 3–12 months: path diagnostics

Primary evidence:

- cumulative return and MaxDD;
- realized volatility;
- monthly hit rate and downside-month frequency;
- turnover and realized slippage versus the 10 bp/side assumption;
- concentration of gains by symbol and by month;
- difference between Production next-open fills and model next-open fills.

CAGR is descriptive only. Do not retune the strategy from this sample.

### 12–24 months: first performance review

Use annualized return only with the path diagnostics above. A RED condition is triggered if CAGR is negative and MaxDD is at or below -30%. MaxDD at or below -40% is RED regardless of horizon. MaxDD at or below -30% without the RED combination remains AMBER.

Do not compare directly against the historical 50.65% after-tax CAGR as a pass/fail hurdle.

### 24–36 months: Forward viability review

The pre-registered 24-month hurdle is after-tax CAGR 20%.

- If gross OOS CAGR is already below 20%, the after-tax hurdle is impossible and the strategy is RED.
- If gross CAGR is above 20% but exact after-tax OOS CAGR is not yet available, retain AMBER until exact after-tax calculation is available.
- Continue winner-concentration, turnover, slippage, and MaxDD diagnostics.

### 36+ months: Forward40 decision window

The pre-registered 36-month hurdle is after-tax CAGR 30% as a minimum viability gate, not the final 40% objective.

- If gross OOS CAGR is below 30%, the strategy is RED.
- Otherwise evaluate exact after-tax CAGR and the full path diagnostics.
- The Forward40 objective is considered supported only if the accumulated evidence can justify an after-tax Forward distribution whose median is at least 40%; historical backtest CAGR cannot substitute for this evidence.

## Action states

- GREEN: continue the frozen Fixed60 rule and normal Next Action.
- AMBER: continue the frozen rule, perform a formal review, and prohibit OOS-driven re-optimization.
- RED: stop new entries; if invested, exit all positions at the next US open and move to Cash. Any replacement strategy must be evaluated separately rather than tuned on the failed OOS sample.

## Reporting cadence

Record a formal checkpoint at approximately 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, and 36 months. Interim dashboard updates may be continuous, but they do not change the pre-registered interpretation horizon.

Every checkpoint should report both raw OOS statistics and implementation-integrity diagnostics. Tax status must be explicit: gross values are labeled 税引前; exact tax-adjusted values are labeled 税引後.

## Anti-selection-bias rule

No threshold, factor, allocation, stop, recovery parameter, or Universe rule may be changed because of the observed OOS path and then evaluated on that same OOS history as if it were independent evidence. Any modified strategy starts a new forward clock.
