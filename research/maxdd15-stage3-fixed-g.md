# MaxDD 15 — Stage 3 Fixed60 + G risk-budget portfolios

This stage uses already-observed historical diversification between Fixed60 and frozen Candidate G. It is therefore portfolio-design exploration on the same sample, not independent evidence.

Compare four round allocations, all with residual cash:
- P1: 45% Fixed60 / 0% G / 55% cash
- P2: 42% Fixed60 / 18% G / 40% cash
- P3: 39% Fixed60 / 26% G / 35% cash
- P4: 35% Fixed60 / 35% G / 30% cash

No Fixed60 or G rule is changed. The purpose is to see whether G diversification can raise CAGR around the ~15% historical MaxDD target relative to static Fixed60 scaling.
